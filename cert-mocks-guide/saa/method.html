How to take SAA mocks.

Treat each mock like Pearson Vue: one pass, flag hard stems, no tab-hopping into answers. Domains: Secure 30% · Resilient 26% · High-performing 24% · Cost 20%.

01 Session rules

Start the timer, answer all 65, then score — explanations teach after you commit.

  • Budget ~2 min/question; skip sinkholes and return.
  • Underline constraints (RPO, single-AZ, compliance, cost ceiling).
  • Eliminate two distractors before picking.
  • After score: open every wrong answer’s lesson in aws-saa-guide.
