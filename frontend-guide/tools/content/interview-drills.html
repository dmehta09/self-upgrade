Interview · under the clock

Timed frontend drills

Interviews are judgment under time pressure. Three chip-quiz modes drill the calls this guide teaches — where code runs, where state lives, and what fixes the bug — with a countdown, streaks, and a best score that sticks around.

00 How a round works

The clock
Each question gives you 30–45 seconds — the bar drains, and a timeout counts as a miss. Speed is the point: these are reflexes, not research.
The chips
Read the scenario, click the right chip. Boundary and tool chips keep fixed positions between questions so you get faster every round.
The traps
Every question has a designed-in wrong answer. Fall for it and the verdict explains the trap, not just the right call.
The score
Misses link back to the owning lesson; your best score per mode persists in your browser (localStorage, nothing leaves your machine).
Keyboard faster

Press 1–9 to answer, Enter or → for the next question, Esc to bail back to the intro. No mouse needed.

01 Server or client?

02 Pick the tool

Where should this state or data live — useState, TanStack Query, Zustand, the URL, a form library, context, a Server Action, or no state at all? The decision guide, on a clock.

03 Debug it

A symptom from production, eight candidate fixes, one right answer. Stale closures, identity leaks, response races, and cache lies — every gotcha in this guide, weaponized.
