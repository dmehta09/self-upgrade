OpenTelemetry · module overview

OpenTelemetry — one standard.

Before OTel, every vendor shipped its own agent, SDK, and wire format. Swapping backends meant re-instrumenting your whole codebase. OTel ended that: one vendor-neutral standard for traces, metrics, logs — and now profiles — that any backend can consume.
