SLO math — commit to memory

SLOMonthly budget14.4× burn = page in
99.9%43.2 min downtime / 0.1% errors~50 hours at 1h window
99.5%3.6 hours / 0.5% errors~10 days at 1h window
99.99%4.3 min / 0.01% errors~5 hours at 1h window

Formula: budget = (100 − SLO%) × window. 14.4× burn on 1h = exhausts budget in ~50h for 99.9%.
