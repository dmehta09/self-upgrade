01 The journey: can’t-debug-it → on-call lead

You don’t level up by memorizing tools — you level up by understanding what each signal answers and what it costs. Find your level, then close the gaps. Every chip is one page.

In plain English analogy

Becoming a doctor for your systems. A junior reads the obvious vitals; a mid orders the right tests when something’s off; a senior diagnoses a never-seen illness from the symptoms and writes the protocol so the team can too.

02 The one mental model: three signals, one pipeline

Almost everything in observability hangs off one picture — telemetry flowing from your code to a place you can ask it questions. Keep this in your head; every topic is one box on it.

instrument → collect → store → see
1 · instrument
OpenTelemetry
logs · metrics · traces
2 · collect
Collector / Alloy
batch · sample · route (OTLP)
3 · store
Prometheus · Loki · Tempo
time series · logs · traces
4 · see
Grafana · alerts
dashboards · SLOs · paging
Instrument once with OpenTelemetry; the Collector decouples your app from whatever backend stores and shows the data.
