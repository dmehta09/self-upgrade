01 The deck

Twenty high-yield conversions, formulas, and method cues. Drill them daily until each front instantly fires the back.

0/0 known

  • 12.5% as a fraction?

    1/8 — divide by 8.

  • 33⅓% as a fraction?

    1/3.

  • Up a%, then down a% — net change?

    A loss of (a²/100)%. (20% → −4%.)

  • Convert km/h to m/s?

    × 5/18. (Reverse: × 18/5.)

  • Average speed for equal distances at x and y?

    2xy/(x+y) — the harmonic mean, not (x+y)/2.

  • Simple interest formula?

    SI = P·R·T / 100.

  • Compound amount formula?

    A = P(1 + R/100)ⁿ.

  • Profit % is taken on…?

    Cost price. (Discount is on marked price.)

  • HCF × LCM of two numbers = ?

    The product of the two numbers (a × b).

  • nPr vs nCr — which cares about order?

    nPr (permutation). nCr (combination) ignores order.

  • Time & work: combine workers by adding…?

    Their rates (1/a + 1/b), never their days.

  • Alligation gives you the…?

    Ratio of the two parts (not the amounts). cheaper:dearer = (d−m):(m−c).

  • Clock angle formula?

    |30H − 5.5M| degrees.

  • Painted n³ cube — how many have 3 painted faces?

    Always 8 (the corners).

  • Two common Pythagorean triples?

    3-4-5 and 5-12-13 (also 8-15-17).

  • "Some A are B." Does "Some A are not B" follow?

    No — it's only a possibility, not a certainty.

  • Turning right rotates you which way?

    Clockwise: N → E → S → W.

  • Pie chart: 1% equals how many degrees?

    3.6°. (share% × 3.6 = degrees.)

  • Data sufficiency asks you to find…?

    Whether the statements are enough — not the actual value.

  • Calendar: "odd days" means…?

    The remainder when the day count is divided by 7.
