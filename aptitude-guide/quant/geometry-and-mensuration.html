Geometry & mensuration.

Easy marks if you know the formula and a Pythagorean triple. Most OA geometry is area/perimeter/volume plus a right triangle — not olympiad proofs.

01 The idea

Mensuration is plug-in formulas. Geometry on OA papers is usually right triangles + circles. Memorise the sheet; don't derive under the clock.

You're graded on speed: recognise the shape, write the formula, cancel units, pick the option. Diagrams are often drawn to scale — but never trust a sketch for exact angles unless labelled.

In plain English analogy

Think of a carpentry cheat sheet taped to your bench: for each shape you already know which measurement to multiply. A rectangle is length × breadth; a circle is πr². The exam is "which shape is this, and which line is the radius?" — not inventing new maths.

Three shapes that cover most OA area questions — rectangle, triangle, circle.

Pro tip tip

If a right triangle has sides 6 and 8, hypotenuse is 10 (3-4-5 ×2). Don't compute √(36+64) under pressure.

03 Classic traps

Trap

Use diameter as radius in πr². Or confuse perimeter with area. Or forget ½ on triangle area.

Fix

Label r vs d on the diagram. Ask: "am I measuring edge length or enclosed space?" For triangles, say "half base height" out loud.

Common trap gotcha

A wire of length L bent into a circle has circumference L = 2πr, so r = L/(2π). Area is πr² — not πL². Students square the wire length by mistake.

A right triangle has legs 9 and 12. Hypotenuse?

15. 9-12-15 is 3-4-5 scaled by 3. Spot the triple before reaching for a calculator.

04 Solve it fast

Area of a circle with diameter 14 (use π = 22/7)?warm-up
Solve it

r = 7. Area = πr² = (22/7)·49 = 22·7 = 154.

The fast way shortcut

Halve the diameter first. With π = 22/7 and r = 7, the 7s cancel cleanly.

A rectangular field is 40 m by 30 m. Cost of fencing at ₹20/m?core
Solve it

Perimeter = 2(40+30) = 140 m. Cost = 140 × 20 = ₹2800.

The fast way shortcut

Fencing = perimeter, not area. 2(l+b) then × rate.

Volume of a cylinder: r = 7 cm, h = 10 cm (π = 22/7)?stretch
Solve it

V = πr²h = (22/7)·49·10 = 22·7·10 = 1540 cm³.

The fast way shortcut

Same cancel as the circle: (22/7)·49 = 154, then ×10.
