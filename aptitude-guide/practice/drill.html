Practice · timed drill

Timed practice drill.

The whole point of aptitude prep: answer under the clock. Pick a section (or mix everything), choose how many questions, and race — you get instant scoring, a per-question review with worked explanations, and your best score saved.

01 How it works

1

Pick a section & count

All sections mixed, Quant / Reasoning / Verbal / DI, or Stretch only for the harder OA pool. Choose 10, 20, or 30 questions.

2

Race the clock

About 45 seconds per question. The timer turns amber, then red — practising under pressure is the point.

3

Answer & learn

Pick an option to see the correct answer and a one-line explanation immediately, then advance.

4

Review & beat your best

End with a score, accuracy, and time-per-question, plus a review of every question. Your best run is saved in this browser.

Keyboard friendly tip

Press 1–4 to pick an option and Enter to advance — keep your hands on the keys and your eyes on the clock.

02 The arena

Open this page in a browser to run the timed drill — choose a section and count, answer against the clock, and get an instant score with explanations. (JavaScript required for the live timer.)

03 Practise like it counts

Should I review the misses or just keep drilling?core
Do this

Always open the review and read the explanation for every wrong answer — the gain is in understanding the trap, not in raw reps. Then re-drill the same topic page until those misses turn green.

The fast way shortcut

Wrong answer → read why → re-drill that topic. Speed without review just makes the same mistake faster.

What score should I aim for?warm-up
Do this

Push for 85%+ accuracy at ~45 s a question on a mixed 20-question run. Hit that consistently and you'll comfortably clear most placement cut-offs. Weak topic? Drill it on its own page first.

The fast way shortcut

Target 85% at 45 s/Q on a mixed set. Accuracy first, then squeeze the clock.
