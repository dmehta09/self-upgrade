01 The whole book in one sentence

The greatest limit on what we build is our ability to understand what we've built — so the job of design is to minimise complexity, continually.

In plain English analogy

Complexity is clutter in a workshop. No single stray tool stops you, but let it pile up and every job gets slower and riskier. Good design is the discipline of putting tools back — so the next person (often future-you) can work fast.
