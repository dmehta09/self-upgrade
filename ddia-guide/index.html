First, one idea you'll use everywhere: the average lies

Data systems are judged on the slow few percent of requests, not the mean. Drag the control: as a request fans out to more backends, its tail latency climbs — because it's only as fast as its slowest call. This little engine reappears throughout the guide, drawn for whatever it's teaching.
