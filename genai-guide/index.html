Think of a brilliant new hire analogy

The model is a brilliant, well-read intern who has read most of the internet but joined the company yesterday — so they know general things but nothing about your world. Prompting is how you brief them. RAG is handing them the right documents before they answer. Tools & agents let them actually do things — search, run code, file a ticket. Fine-tuning is sending them on a training course. And evaluation, serving & safety are the manager making sure the work is good, fast, and safe. This guide teaches every one of those jobs.
