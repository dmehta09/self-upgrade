Senior Backend · Scenario Q&A

120 production scenarios.
Answers you can say out loud.

A rehearsal guide for senior backend interviews — FastAPI-first with Nest/Node contrast notes. Every card has a 30-second open, a stepwise model answer, traps, and follow-ups. Built from the must-practice PDF bank; structured so later packs drop into the same banks.

01  How to use this guide

1 · Learn the method

Read Answer method once — clarify → hypothesize → evidence → fix → prevent. Every domain card follows that arc.

2 · Closed-book domain pass

Pick a domain. Cover the answer. Speak your structure for 60–90s. Then open the card and score yourself honestly.

3 · Rapid-fire + flashcards

Use drills and flashcards the day before interviews. “Say it” lines are the deck.
