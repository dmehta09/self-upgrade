Method

How to answer scenario questions

Interviewers are grading your structure under ambiguity, not a memorized wiki page. Use the same five-beat loop on every card in this guide.

01  The five-beat loop

1 · Clarify
Restate the symptom, environment, and success criteria in one sentence. Ask one sharp question if a fact is missing (SLA? blast radius? recent deploy?).
2 · Hypothesize
Name 2–3 ranked failure modes. Seniors prune — they don’t brainstorm twenty ideas.
3 · Evidence
Say what you’d look at next and why: which log, metric, trace, health check, or EXPLAIN. Tie each check to one hypothesis.
4 · Fix
Propose the smallest safe mitigation first (shed load, rollback, open circuit), then the durable fix.
5 · Prevent
Close with the control that stops recurrence: alert, test, timeout budget, runbook, idempotency key.
Say it spoken

“I’d clarify the symptom and blast radius, rank two or three causes, pick the next evidence hop that falsifies them, mitigate safely, then leave a prevention — alert, test, or design change.”

02  When it’s a design question

Swap “hypothesize/evidence” for constraints → API/data → failure modes → observability. Always name the consistency and idempotency story. Prefer boring defaults (timeouts, retries with jitter, outbox) over novel protocols unless asked.

04  Self-check

What’s the weakest opening on a production incident question?
Fixes without a framed problem signal panic. Clarify → rank causes → evidence → mitigate → prevent.
