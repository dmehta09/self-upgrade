How to think about any coding problem

Strong candidates don't memorise 500 problems. They learn to recognise which of a dozen patterns a question is really asking for. Almost everything in this guide is one step of this picture — keep it in mind.

Steps 2 and 3 are the whole game. Learn the patterns and the right data structure, and the code almost writes itself.
Think of a toolbox analogy

Imagine a carpenter with a small toolbox. They don't own a different tool for every job — they own a saw, a hammer, a drill, and they know which one a job needs. A pattern is a tool. There are only about a dozen. Once you can look at a problem and think "that's a job for the two-pointers tool," you've done the hard part.
