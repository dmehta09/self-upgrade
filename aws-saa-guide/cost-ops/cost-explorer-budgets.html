Cost & Operations · Explorer, Budgets & tags Domain 4 · Cost-optimized

See the bill. Cap the bill. Own the bill.

Cost Explorer explains spend. Budgets alerts when you cross a line. Allocation tags (and CUR when you need a warehouse) assign dollars to teams. Without visibility, every other cost win is guessing.

01 The idea

Explore first to find the spike, set Budgets so it cannot silently grow, then activate tags so chargeback knows who owns the dollars.

In plain English analogy

Cost Explorer is your bank app's spending categories. Budgets are the text that says "you've hit 80% of groceries." Tags are the names on the shared card so you know who bought what.

flowchart TD
  spike["Bill looks wrong"] --> explorer["Cost Explorer · filter & group"]
  explorer --> find["Find service · account · tag"]
  find --> budgets["AWS Budgets · thresholds"]
  budgets --> alert["SNS / email at 50% · 80% · 100%"]
  find --> tags["Activate cost allocation tags"]
  tags --> own["Chargeback by Team / Project"]

02 Spike Tuesday & friends

Named stories beat abstract menus. Match the stem to investigate, alert, or allocate.

Acme · Spike Tuesday
Finance sees a mid-month jump. Open Cost Explorer, group by service → NAT data transfer. Fix with VPC endpoints; add a Budgets alert so next spike pages FinOps.
Nova apps
Each product team needs its own monthly target. Activate Team / Project cost allocation tags; Budgets filtered by tag; Explorer for variance reviews.
Ledger Co BI
CFO wants multi-year line items in QuickSight. Deliver CUR to S3 → Athena/QuickSight. Explorer stays the human UI for day-to-day digs.
Cost Explorer
  • Interactive charts & filters
  • Forecasts, rightsizing hints
  • Human investigation of spikes
  • Fast answers in the console
CUR (Cost & Usage Report)
  • Line-item detail to S3
  • Athena / QuickSight / custom BI
  • Multi-year warehouse analysis
  • When Explorer's UI is not enough

03 Budgets, tags & gotchas

Budgets watch cost, usage, or Savings Plans / RI coverage. Tags only allocate after you activate them in Billing.

Good Budgets use
  • Alert at 50% / 80% / 100% forecasted
  • Per account or tagged project
  • Usage budgets for transfer or Spot
Weak use
  • One org-wide budget, no owners
  • Alert-only with nobody on-call
  • Blind auto-deny with no exception path
Common trap gotcha

Tags only show in Cost Explorer after you activate cost allocation tags in Billing (can take ~24h). Untagged spend dumps into "no tag." Also: Budgets ≠ Cost Anomaly Detection — different product for unusual spikes.

Leadership wants each product team charged for its own AWS spend and alerted at 80% of monthly target. What do you enable?

Right. Chargeback needs activated allocation tags (or account-per-team) plus Budgets for thresholds; Explorer explains variance. CPU alarms and one RI do not allocate cost.

04 Exam rapid-fire

Cost Explorer vs Budgets?warm-up
Model answer

Explorer analyzes historical and forecasted spend. Budgets watches thresholds and notifies (or acts). You usually need both: explore to understand, budget to stay honest.

Say it out loud say it

"Explorer is the microscope; Budgets is the smoke alarm."

Why don't my tags show in Cost Explorer?core
Model answer

They must be marked as cost allocation tags in Billing (and can take up to ~24h). Tagging the resource alone is not enough for chargeback reports.

Say it out loud say it

"Tag the resource, then activate the tag for billing — two steps."

When reach for CUR?core
Model answer

When you need line-item detail, custom BI, or multi-year analysis beyond Explorer's UI — deliver CUR to S3 and query with Athena/QuickSight.

Say it out loud say it

"Explorer for humans; CUR for warehouses."
