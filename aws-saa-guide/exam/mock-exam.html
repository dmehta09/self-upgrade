Exam prep · timed mock

30-minute SAA-style mock.

Twenty domain-weighted questions (~5 Secure, ~5 Resilient, ~5 High-performing, ~5 Cost). Start the timer, pick one answer each, then score — each question shows why and a lesson link.

01 Take the mock

Treat it like the real exam: no peeking at notes. After scoring, use domain bars and the per-question review to choose the next lesson from the domain map.

02 After you score

  • Secure weak → IAM, KMS, endpoints.
  • Resilient weak → Multi-AZ, DR, decoupling.
  • Perf weak → ELB, S3, ElastiCache.
  • Cost weak → pricing, Budgets.

Then drill the scenario bank and flashcards.
