Foundations · SageMaker vs Bedrock

Train a custom model or call a foundation model?

MLA-C02 loves this fork: SageMaker when you need full control over algorithms, features, and artifacts; Bedrock when a managed FM plus prompt/RAG/fine-tune is enough. JumpStart is the middle ground — pretrained models you deploy or fine-tune without building from scratch.

01 The chooser

Start from the constraint: proprietary tabular fraud model with engineered features → SageMaker. Policy Q&A over PDFs with low ops → Bedrock + Knowledge Bases. Image classifier with a known pretrained backbone → JumpStart.

flowchart TD
  start["New ML need"] --> type{"Problem type?"}
  type -->|tabular / classic ML| sm["SageMaker train + endpoint"]
  type -->|GenAI text / image FM| bed{"Need custom weights?"}
  bed -->|no · RAG / prompt| br["Bedrock invoke + KB"]
  bed -->|light customize| peft["Bedrock fine-tune / Continued PT"]
  bed -->|heavy custom| smft["SageMaker fine-tune OR JumpStart"]
  type -->|known pretrained| js["JumpStart deploy / adapt"]
In plain English analogy

Bedrock is hiring a specialist consultant by the hour — fast, managed, great for language tasks. SageMaker Training is building your own specialist from your hospital's records. JumpStart is hiring a consultant who already took a course in your domain and only needs a short onboarding.

02 Ledger Bank vs Acme Health

Same AWS account, different forks. Ledger's credit fraud model uses hand-crafted transaction features — SageMaker. Acme's discharge-summary chatbot uses Claude on Bedrock with a Knowledge Base over clinical guidelines.

Ledger Bank · fraud scoring
  • Millions of rows, strict latency SLA
  • Custom feature pipeline + XGBoost
  • Model Registry + real-time endpoint
  • Pick: SageMaker Training + Feature Store
Acme Health · policy assistant
  • Unstructured PDFs, natural language answers
  • RAG over internal protocols
  • Guardrails for PHI-safe responses
  • Pick: Bedrock + Knowledge Bases

03 JumpStart — the middle ground

JumpStart bundles pretrained models, notebooks, and deploy scripts inside SageMaker. Use it when the exam mentions pretrained but still needs SageMaker endpoints — not Bedrock FMs.

JumpStart
Curated models (Hugging Face, built-ins) + one-click deploy/fine-tune in SageMaker.
Bedrock
AWS-managed FMs (Claude, Llama, Titan, etc.) with unified API, KB, Agents, Guardrails.
SageMaker Training
Bring your own script/container; full pipeline control and Model Registry integration.
Common trap gotcha

Bedrock for classical tabular fraud. If the stem describes engineered numeric features and a gradient-boosted classifier, Bedrock is wrong — even if "AI" is in the job title. That's SageMaker territory.

Ledger Bank needs real-time fraud scores from 200 engineered features with full lineage. Best starting point?

Right. Tabular fraud with custom features → SageMaker classical ML stack. Bedrock is for FM/GenAI patterns, not replacing a feature-rich classifier.

04 Exam rapid-fire

When is Bedrock the right default?warm-up
Model answer

GenAI workloads: text generation, summarization, chat, RAG over documents, agents — especially when the stem wants managed FMs and minimal training infrastructure.

Say it out loud say it

"Bedrock when I need a foundation model served for me — not when I'm training XGBoost on fraud features."

What does JumpStart buy you?core
Model answer

Pretrained models and fine-tuning recipes inside SageMaker — faster than training from scratch, more control than calling Bedrock FMs. Good for vision/NLP deploy scenarios that still say "SageMaker endpoint."

Say it out loud say it

"JumpStart = pretrained in SageMaker — middle ground between custom train and Bedrock FM."

Stem: 'Q&A over internal PDFs, least operational overhead.' Eliminate two options.stretch
Model answer

Reject: (1) training a custom LLM from scratch on SageMaker, (2) self-hosting an open-weight model on EC2. Keep: Bedrock + Knowledge Base (RAG) with optional Guardrails.

Say it out loud say it

"PDF Q&A + least ops → Bedrock RAG; kill DIY LLM training and raw EC2 hosting."
