Operate · cost & accelerators

Right-size training, cheap inference, smart FM spend.

ML bills come from always-on endpoints, oversized training instances, and unbounded Bedrock tokens. MLA-C02 pairs cost constraints with the right accelerator or pricing model — Spot for fault-tolerant train, Inferentia for inference, token caps for GenAI.

01 Training cost levers

Use Spot Training with checkpointing for long jobs. Right-size instance family; use managed Spot in SageMaker Training. HPO parallel jobs multiply cost — cap concurrent trials.

On-Demand training
  • Predictable, no interruption
  • Higher $/hour
  • Short jobs / final prod train
Spot / Managed Spot
  • Up to ~90% discount
  • Checkpoint to S3 on interrupt
  • Exam: fault-tolerant, not latency-critical

02 Inferentia, Trainium & GPU

AWS AI chips: Trainium for training throughput, Inferentia/Inferentia2 for low-cost inference on SageMaker and EC2. Exam stem “reduce inference cost at scale” → Neo compile + Inf instances before defaulting to p4d GPU.

03 Bedrock token economics

On-demand = pay per input/output token. Provisioned throughput when steady high volume. Reduce tokens: shorter prompts, RAG top-k limits, caching (where supported), smaller FM for routing tasks.

Ledger Bank tip

Ledger routes simple intent classification to a small FM and escalates to a large FM only when confidence is low — cuts average tokens per customer chat by half.

04 Endpoint right-sizing

Over-provision trap
  • ml.p3.2xlarge 24/7 for 10 req/min
  • Serverless or smaller Inf instance
Fix
  • Auto scaling on InvocationsPerInstance
  • Multi-model endpoint share GPU
  • Batch for offline scoring
