JumpStart vs train from scratch.

SageMaker JumpStart ships pretrained models, notebooks, and fine-tuning recipes. MLA stems contrast JumpStart fine-tune with Bedrock FMs — custom ML on SageMaker vs managed GenAI API.

01 JumpStart pretrained & fine-tune

JumpStart gives pretrained weights (vision, NLP, tabular embeddings) plus one-click fine-tune/deploy. Fine-tune when your label distribution differs from pretraining; train from scratch only when data is huge and domain is far from public corpora — rare on the exam.

02 JumpStart vs Bedrock

JumpStart = you own training jobs, containers, endpoints, and classic ML/ DL lifecycle on SageMaker. Bedrock = invoke foundation models via API; Knowledge Bases, Agents, Guardrails — least ops for GenAI text.

Choose JumpStart when…
  • Custom vision/tabular DL with your weights
  • Model Registry / Pipeline promotion required
  • Inference on SageMaker endpoints (GPU you control)
  • Non-GenAI or fine-tuned DL outside Bedrock catalog
Choose Bedrock when…
  • Chat, summarization, RAG over docs
  • No container management — API + KB + Agents
  • Provisioned throughput for steady FM traffic
  • Guardrails and responsible GenAI built-in
Common trap gotcha

JumpStart for a chatbot over PDF policies. That’s Bedrock + Knowledge Base territory — JumpStart fine-tune is for when you need a deployable SageMaker model artifact, not a managed FM API.

03 Exam quiz

Acme Health wants a nurse-facing chatbot answering policy PDFs with citations, minimal ML ops. Best path?

Right. GenAI Q&A with citations → Bedrock RAG. JumpStart image models and from-scratch transformers miss the GenAI + least-ops constraint.
