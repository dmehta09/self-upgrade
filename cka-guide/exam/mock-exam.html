Timed task mock.

Simulate CKA pacing. Start the timer, work tasks on your lab cluster, mark done when verified. Reveal solutions only after you submit.

01 Instructions

This is a self-scored performance drill — real graders check the live cluster.

Race with a map, not a passenger analogy

In plain English: the timer is the race clock; the solution reveal is the map you open after the stage. Peeking mid-stage trains the wrong muscle.

  • Budget ~6–8 minutes average; skip sinkholes and return.
  • Domain mix matches CKA weight: more Troubleshooting, Cluster, Networking.
  • Use only docs/patterns you would have on exam day.
  • Mark done only after your own verify step passes.
Gotcha · marking done without verify

Self-check score is only as honest as your checkboxes. Curl, Ready, Bound, can-i — then tick.
