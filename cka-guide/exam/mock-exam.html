01 Full mock run

Domain-weighted toward Troubleshooting, Cluster, and Networking. Use a disposable cluster; solutions are command sketches, not unique answers.

02 After the run

What score should you treat as a pass rehearsal? Core

Say it out loud: Mark at least sixty-six percent done under the two-hour timer, with Troubleshooting items verified.

Answer

Official pass ≥66%. Self-check marks are not a grader — still, timed ≥66% with heavy domains solid is the rehearsal bar.

How do you spend the last ten minutes? Warm

Say it out loud: Verify only — Ready replicas, Endpoints, NetworkPolicies, node Ready. No new features.

Answer

Re-check high-weight tasks. Partial correct state can still score; unfinished heroics usually do not.
