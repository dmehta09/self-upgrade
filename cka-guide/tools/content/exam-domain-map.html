CKA domain map.

Weights are the study schedule. Troubleshooting alone is nearly a third of the score — map hours and practice tasks to domains before you grind YAML.

01 Five domains

Pass bar is ≥66%. Miss heavy domains and no amount of Storage trivia saves you.

Budget hours like exam points analogy

In plain English: treat each domain like a line item on a project plan. Put the biggest slice of lab time where the graders put the biggest slice of score — then fill Storage last.

DomainWeightWhat you practiceGuide module
Troubleshooting 30% Pods, nodes, control plane, Services/DNS/NP, logs & debug, resources Troubleshooting
Cluster Architecture, Installation & Configuration 25% kubeadm, control plane & nodes, CRI/CNI/CSI, RBAC, Helm/Kustomize, CRDs, HA lifecycle Cluster
Services & Networking 20% Pod network, Services/Endpoints, NetworkPolicies, Ingress, Gateway API, CoreDNS Networking
Workloads & Scheduling 15% Deployments/rollouts, ConfigMaps/Secrets, probes, requests/limits/HPA, affinity/taints Workloads
Storage 10% Volumes & access modes, PV/PVC lifecycle, StorageClass & dynamic provision Storage

03 Study loop

One weekly cycle beats cramming the night before. Close every loop with a timed verify.

1

Learn

Read one lesson; draw the control-plane → kubelet path once. Keep kubectl docs open.

2

Drill

Run 2–3 tasks from the task bank in that domain. Constraint → commands → verify.

3

Time

Weekly: one half-mock or full timed mock. Protect Troubleshooting minutes.

4

Recall

Flip flashcards and skim the cheat-sheet for gaps only.

flowchart TD
  L[Lesson] --> D[Task bank drill]
  D --> V{Verify Ready / Endpoints?}
  V -->|no| L
  V -->|yes| M[Timed mock slice]
  M --> F[Flashcards · weak domains]
  F --> L
Gotcha · equal chapter fallacy

Five domains does not mean 20% each. Skipping Troubleshooting for “more YAML” is how strong labbers still miss the cut score.

Which domain should get the largest share of timed practice?

Domain 1 is 30% — the highest weight in CKA Curriculum v1.35.

04 Quick check

Recite the five weights in order of size. Core

Say it out loud: thirty, twenty-five, twenty, fifteen, ten — Troubleshooting, Cluster, Networking, Workloads, Storage.

Answer

Troubleshooting 30% · Cluster Architecture 25% · Services & Networking 20% · Workloads & Scheduling 15% · Storage 10%.

Where do Gateway API and Helm land? Warm

Say it out loud: Gateway with Networking; Helm and Kustomize with Cluster configuration.

Answer

Gateway API (and Ingress) → Services & Networking. Helm/Kustomize → Cluster Architecture, Installation & Configuration.

Is deep etcd backup a core CKA drill? Warm

Say it out loud: No — focus on control-plane health, kubeadm lifecycle, and app/node troubleshooting instead.

Answer

Curriculum no longer centers deep etcd dump/restore. Know that etcd holds state; practice fixing apiserver, kubelet, and workloads.
