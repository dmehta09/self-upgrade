Storage · PV & PVC Domain 5 · Storage (10%)

PV & PVC lifecycle.

Apps claim storage with a PersistentVolumeClaim. The cluster binds a matching PersistentVolume, then the kubelet mounts it. CKA tasks often stop at Pending until you fix binding.

01 The idea

PVC = what the app needs (size, access mode, class). PV = actual storage. Binding matches capacity, modes, StorageClass, and selectors.

Analogy plain English

A PVC is a housing application (size, neighborhood, pets allowed). A PV is a vacant apartment. Binding is the lease signing. Mount is getting the keys into your Pod’s hallway — claim → bind → mount.

PersistentVolume
Cluster-scoped; describes capacity, accessModes, reclaimPolicy, and how to reach storage.
PersistentVolumeClaim
Namespaced; requests storage; Pods reference the claim name.
Bound
PVC and PV are paired; Pod can then mount the claim.

02 Claim → bind → mount

Static provisioning: you create the PV first. Dynamic: a StorageClass provisions the PV when the PVC appears. Either way the kubelet finishes the mount on the scheduled node.

pvc.yaml 
apiVersion: v1
kind: PersistentVolumeClaim
metadata:
  name: app-data
  namespace: demo
spec:
  accessModes:
  - ReadWriteOnce
  resources:
    requests:
      storage: 1Gi
  storageClassName: local-path   # "" for static-only match</span>

03 Exam kubectl

Always read PVC Events when Pending. Check storageClassName, accessModes, capacity, and whether a PV exists or a provisioner is running.

Gotcha exam trap

PVC with a StorageClass name that doesn’t exist (or storageClassName: "" when only dynamic PVs exist) never binds. Deleting a PVC with reclaimPolicy Retain leaves an orphaned PV — Released, not Available — until you clear claimRef.

PVC is Bound but the Pod is ContainerCreating with FailedMount. Where do you look next?

Binding succeeded; mount is the kubelet/CSI node step. describe Pod Events usually names Multi-Attach, timeout, or missing CSI driver.

04 Reclaim & phases

Know Available → Bound → Released. ReclaimPolicy: Retain (keep data), Delete (provisioner cleans up), Recycle (legacy — don’t rely on it).

05 Exam rapid-fire

Walk claim → bind → mount in one breath. core

Create PVC → controller binds or provisions a PV → Pod references the claim → kubelet mounts on the node.

Say it out loud

“Claim asks, bind pairs a PV, mount puts it in the container.”

PVC Pending forever — checklist? core

storageClassName, accessModes, requested size vs PV capacity, volumeBindingMode WaitForFirstConsumer (needs a scheduled Pod), provisioner logs, no matching static PV.

Say it out loud

“I describe the PVC before I rewrite YAML — Events usually name the mismatch.”

Retain after PVC delete — what do you do? stretch

PV is Released. To reuse: edit/patch the PV to remove claimRef (or recreate a PVC that can rebind per policy), confirm data still on the backend, then remount.

Say it out loud

“Retain keeps the disk; Released means I must clear claimRef before it binds again.”
